About RubyJust Cafe

Delighting dessert lovers across Thailand with passion, quality, and sweet inspiration for over 5 years.

Our Story & Inspiration

RubyJust Cafe was born out of a deep personal passion for cakes and baking. Inspired by the cozy, aesthetically charming, and delightfully balanced flavors of Korean cafe culture, our founder dreamed of bringing that unique sweet experience right to Thailand. Over the past 5 years, we have grown from a small baking project into a beloved destination for dessert enthusiasts.

We believe that a great cake should not only look stunning but also have an inviting taste that feels accessible to everyone. That is why we carefully select premium, high-quality ingredients — from fresh seasonal fruits to gourmet chocolate and imported dairy — ensuring every bite is pure perfection.

With a wide variety of menu items and flexible price ranges to suit every occasion, RubyJust Cafe proudly offers everything from classic shortcakes and rich fudges to custom celebration designs, welcoming everyone into our warm sanctuary of sweets.
